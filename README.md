# Salary Prediction Based on Country and Race

## ✦ Project Overview
This project aims to predict the salary of individuals from various countries and racial backgrounds based on their demographic attributes, including occupation, age, gender, experience, and education. The dataset provides a comprehensive collection of salary and demographic information, offering a valuable resource for studying the relationship between income and various socio-demographic factors. 

## ✦ Problem Statement
Understanding income diversity and the multifaceted factors that influence earning potential in today's workforce. The project focuses on exploring patterns and trends in income distribution across different demographic categories to identify potential disparities and examine how income varies based on both demographic characteristics and accumulated work experience.

## ✦ Goals
* **Data Preprocessing:** Clean the dataset by removing null values, dropping unnecessary index columns, categorizing 191 distinct job titles into broader domains, and grouping similar education levels.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Understand the distribution of data across various demographic features.
* **Visual Analysis:** Visualize the data to understand the relationships between independent features (Age, Gender, Education, Job Title, Experience, Country, Race) and the target variable (Salary).

## ✦ Key Insights from the Data Analysis
* **Demographic Breakdown:** The workforce is predominantly male (54.8%), with females making up 45% and other genders 0.2%. The majority of employees are young professionals between 25 and 35 years old, and most have 0-7 years of experience. 
* **Education & Career:** Most employees hold a Bachelor's degree, indicating that the majority enter the workforce after graduation. Roles like Software Developer, Data Analyst/Scientist, and Manager/Director/VP are in the highest demand.
* **Global & Racial Distribution:** Employee representation is fairly balanced across the UK, USA, Canada, China, and Australia, with a slight majority in the USA. White and Asian demographics represent the largest racial groups in the dataset.
* **Salary Trends by Age & Experience:** There is a clear trend of salary increasing with age, likely due to promotions and appraisals. However, multiple salaries exist for similar ages, indicating that age alone does not determine income.
* **Gender Pay Dynamics:** The 'Other' gender category has a notably high median salary (above $150,000) compared to Males (median ~$107,500) and Females (median ~$100,000). The female salary distribution is the most widely spread, with a high concentration near $50,000.
